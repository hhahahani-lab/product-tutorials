Rules

I. Total Data Description

1. Time Zone: Unified to the agency's time zone.

2. Data Refresh: Refreshes daily at 00:15 and does not include the current day. Data is updated until 23:59:59 of the previous day.

3. Total Data: Combined data of all hosts in my agency.

4. Hosts: Total number of hosts in the agency.

5. Coins: Gift coins received by agency hosts.

6. Consumers: Users who sent gifts to agency hosts.

7. New Hosts: Hosts who signed as a Host for the first time during the selected period.

8. Effective Hosts: Hosts with at least 4 Valid Days and 10 Valid Hours during the selected period.

9. Valid Hours: Total valid on-mic time of agency hosts.

10. Active Hosts: Hosts with on-mic time greater than 0.

11. Gifted Hosts: Hosts who received more than 0 diamonds.

II. Host Data Description

1. Host ID: Search and view data for a specific host in the agency.

2. Time: Select the statistical period, such as This Week or Yesterday.

3. Coins: Gift coins received by the host during the selected period.

4. Consumers: Users who sent gifts to the host during the selected period.

5. Valid Hours: Valid on-mic time of the host during the selected period.

6. Effective Host: Shows Qualified when the host has at least 4 Effective Days and 10 Valid Working Hours during the selected period; otherwise shows Not Qualified.

7. Total Value: Total statistical value used for sorting host data.

8. Sort Order: Supports Descending and Ascending order.
